Patient Continuity Guidelines

The dataset tracks repeat visits over a 12-month timeline. Crucially, the records do not contain scheduled follow-up dates or confirmed follow-up completion. Patients are therefore monitored strictly for longitudinal hospital contact frequency rather than labeled as "missed appointments."
